# Design note: Negotiate a Workable Change (MGT 340, professional sport week, Thursday)

Jeff's lesson prompt for Thursday November 19, 2026 supplied the idea, the roles, the three
things a pod must produce, and the debrief question. This note records the design choices.

1. **The professional and the moment.** A negotiator for a league office, or for a players
   association. The league wants sensors worn in games and the data on the broadcast.
2. **The skill.** Recognise that a change to players' working conditions is negotiated, read
   the operative language before proposing, and adjust a term because another party has a say.
3. **Who holds what.** Each side has private instructions from its principal (the owners'
   labor committee; the board of player representatives). Each negotiator can agree at the
   table to something the principal will not ratify. Neither side sees the other's limits.
4. **What can be verified.** A summary of the wearables section of the agreement in force
   (voluntary in practice, not in games, no use in contract matters, no commercial use). The
   league's three proposed terms. Neither says what the other side will accept.
5. **What a reasonable pod gets wrong.** Management proposes the term as written before
   reading the agreement. Player representatives trade the right to decline for revenue.
   Either side reports that the commissioner or the owners can simply decide.
6. **Who relies on it.** The other side answers the term and move the pod actually chose
   (48 authored answers). The pod's own principal then accepts, corrects or disowns it.
7. **The debrief.** "What changed in the proposal because another party had a meaningful say?"

**Form.** A proposal form and a running exchange, not free chat. The pod writes its own
wording, which is kept on the record, and classifies its move so the answer is rule-decided.

**What is real.** The summary of the current agreement paraphrases Article XXII, Section 13
of the 2023 NBA–NBPA agreement from public sources. Jeff should check it against the
agreement. The proposal, instructions, people and answers are invented.
