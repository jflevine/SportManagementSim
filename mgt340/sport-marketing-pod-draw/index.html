Curveball released. Each pod’s constraint is now on its card. You have three minutes to adjust one of your four Ps.
Debrief questions
  1. Which pod’s locked P forced the most creative move on the other three? What does that tell us about the mix as a system?
  2. Who planned for spectators and who planned for participants? How did that change the product you offered?
  3. Pass the Veeck test: which pod gave people a reason to show up that has nothing to do with winning?
  4. The Clippers and Liverpool draws both involve sponsors. When does a sponsor gain image from a team, and when does it take on risk?
  5. If your pod drew the rival airline, where is the line between clever marketing and ambush marketing?
  6. Which proof metric was most convincing, and which could be gamed?
