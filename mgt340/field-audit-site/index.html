WHAT COUNTS?

A sport operation you can actually observe

Eligible settings include varsity athletics, youth and community sport, professional or amateur events, recreation operations, and live esports events with observable management activity.

Look with six management lenses

  1. Operations & facility
  2. Customer / fan experience
  3. Marketing, revenue & sponsorship
  4. People & management
  5. Access, community & stakeholders
  6. Manager's opportunity

You will need specific field notes and original evidence when you conduct the observation. Avoid disrupting operations or photographing identifiable minors or individuals without permission.

Need an alternative?

Cost, travel, access, work, caregiving, or other circumstances should not prevent you from completing the audit. Request assistance above or contact Professor Levine.
