# Design note: The Investment Committee (MGT 340, professional sport week, Tuesday)

Written before the code, as the authoring guide asks. Jeff's lesson prompt for Tuesday
November 17, 2026 supplied the idea, the two rounds, the debrief question and the grading
standard, so this note records how those were turned into a simulation and what was assumed.

1. **The professional and the moment.** An adviser to the investment committee of a
   league-owned growth fund. The Committee meets at 4:00 PM. Staff has a company to propose.
2. **The skill.** Tell apart what a body can decide alone from what needs someone else's
   consent, and name the document or fact that says so, including when the record does not
   settle the question.
3. **Who holds what.** The Committee chair (can approve up to $50 million; cannot promise
   rights the Fund does not own). Fund counsel (has read only some of the contracts). The
   party that controls the right (a club president, the league's media executive, a stadium
   authority director, or players' union counsel, depending on sector; each can speak only
   for itself). The company's chief executive (knows what smaller deal it would take and how
   long it can wait).
4. **What can be verified.** The charter proves what the Committee may do. The staff memo
   proves who controls the right and says what staff has not confirmed. No document settles
   whether the right-holder will agree. That gap is deliberate.
5. **What a reasonable pod gets wrong.** Approving because $40 million is under the limit.
   Attaching a condition to a party that does not control the right. Walking away without
   asking what smaller deal was available.
6. **Who relies on it.** The chair responds to the recommendation as written. A short
   "afterward" shows what the missing fact turned out to be.
7. **The debrief.** Two pods, same sector, different conditions. "What could your committee
   authorize today, what would it need someone else to agree to, and which document or fact
   tells you that?"

**Form.** Not a conversation: the skill is reading and deciding, so the page is a file and a
recommendation form. Round 1 is recorded as a commitment in the pod's own words before any
document exists, and stays pinned through Round 2 and into the debrief.

**Assumptions to check with Jeff.** Pods of three or four sharing one phone or laptop. The
activity mirrors the printed packets used in class the same day (same four companies, same
charter). Everything in the fiction is invented; nothing describes how the NFL's real
investment vehicle is governed, which is not public.
