      YOUR DECISION TRAIL

      How the simulation works

      You are the President / Managing Partner. Start with your market and board mandate. Then make a defensible allocation decision before uncertainty resolves.

      1. Read — understand the proposal and your mandate.
      2. Compare — use the CFO, Scenario, or Executive Team view when it helps.
      3. Choose — select no more than two investments, or hold cash.
      4. Fund — use existing club cash, or borrow 50% / 70% for eligible projects. Clicking a funding option adds the proposal.
      5. Explain — record the tradeoff and risk you are accepting.
      6. Reveal + review — see the external shock, financial close, and your original reasoning.

      Fictional teaching model. Numbers are simplified and do not represent a specific league, team, CBA, or investment forecast.
