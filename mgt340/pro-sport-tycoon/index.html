      How the simulation works

      You are the President / Managing Partner. Department leaders compete for limited resources. You review their evidence, choose one or two investments, record your rationale, and then see how uncertainty changes the outcome.

      1. Proposal — several investments request funding.
      2. Evidence — open the CFO workbook, scenario model, and department file.
      3. Advice — executives disagree because they optimize different things.
      4. Decision — choose what gets funded and how.
      5. Uncertainty — performance and market conditions resolve after commitment.
      6. Review — compare forecast, actual result, and your original rationale.

      Fictional teaching model. Numbers are simplified and do not represent a specific league, team, CBA, or investment forecast.
