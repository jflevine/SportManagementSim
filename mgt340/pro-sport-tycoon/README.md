# PRO SPORT TYCOON — MGT 340 Week 4

Two-mode browser simulation aligned to Chapter 4, *Financial and Economic Principles Applied to Sport Management*.

## Mode 1: Franchise Office
Five-season professional-franchise simulation. Students manage ticket price, roster investment, fan experience, marketing, commercial focus, and facility capital. Projections appear before decisions are locked; external shocks and board decisions arrive after commitment. Operating profit and cash flow are deliberately separate.

## Mode 2: Financing Room
Capital-project underwriting mini-simulation. Students choose a renovation/new venue/district project, then build a debt/equity/public funding stack. A project shock changes cost or revenues. Students interpret annual debt service, DSCR, simple equity ROI, liquidity pressure, and risk allocation.

## Teaching intent
Revenue → expenses → profit/cash → assets → debt/equity → ROI/risk → league economics/competitive balance.

Fictional, simplified economics for teaching. Not intended to reproduce any real league, club, CBA, or financing structure.
