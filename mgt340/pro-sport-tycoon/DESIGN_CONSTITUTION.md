# PRO SPORT TYCOON — Design Constitution (Condensed)

## Core principle
**You cannot maximize everything at once.**

## Player role
President / Managing Partner.

## Objective
Build a franchise that can **compete, grow, and survive** while balancing operating performance, liquidity, competitive credibility, fan/brand strength, asset value, and future flexibility.

## Core loop
**Proposal → Evidence → Advice → Decision → Uncertainty → Results → Review**

## Non-negotiables
- Scarcity is real.
- Every major choice has an opportunity cost.
- Major choices affect multiple systems.
- Contracts, debt, and capital assets persist.
- Forecasts are not outcomes.
- Executives can disagree without one being “wrong.”
- Every in-game file must contain decision-relevant evidence.
- Decision quality and outcome quality are separate.
- Complexity must remain teachable for an introductory undergraduate course.
- No simple strategy should reliably dominate.

## Main systems
1. Operating P&L
2. Cash / capital structure
3. Revenue portfolio / demand
4. Player / human-capital investment
5. Capital assets / facilities
6. Competitive performance
7. Fan / brand / market
8. Commercial operations
9. Organizational intelligence
10. Risk / uncertainty

## Information architecture
Students can inspect:
- CFO Workbook
- Executive Team recommendations
- Scenario Model
- Department File

## Classroom modes
**Class Mode:** 3 strategic cycles; target 20–25 minutes.  
**Full Mode:** 5 cycles with more path dependence.

## Design test
Success is a student saying:

> “We chose the player because the GM thought our window was now, but the CFO warned us about cash. The injury hurt, but I still think the reasoning was defensible.”

Failure is:

> “We clicked the option because the number turned green.”
