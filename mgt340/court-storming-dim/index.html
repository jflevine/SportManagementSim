FACILITATION

A 15-minute D.I.M. exercise

Students act as the host university’s event manager. Use the 2024 Clark collision as the starting point, then plan for the next game. The later crowd update is a hypothetical.

  1. 3 minutes: Read the short case and AP article.
  2. 6 minutes: Complete Develop, Implement, and Manage.
  3. 3 minutes: Reveal the update and respond.
  4. 3 minutes: Compare plans and debrief.

What to listen for

A specific risk, workable preventive actions, named staff roles, an activation point, clear communication, and a way to check performance. There is no automatic quality score for written work.

Debrief question

If nobody is injured, is that enough evidence that the plan worked?

Invite students to discuss blocked routes, near misses, staff readiness, and whether procedures were followed. Distinguish a written policy from implementation. An incident alone does not establish legal liability.

Collecting work

Students copy or download their completed plan and submit it through Canvas. Print / Save PDF is also available. Responses stay on their own device; this activity has no central submission service or instructor dashboard. No opening or unlocking is required.

This teaching exercise does not claim to reconstruct Ohio State’s actual staffing or establish fault. The schematic is illustrative. The AP source describes a collision without a reported injury.
