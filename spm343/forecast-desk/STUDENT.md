# Forecast Desk: student instructions and text alternative

**Work individually. Start by reviewing the assigned Finch chapter.** Choose one major trend from the reading that you think could shape the future of esports. Explain the trend in your own words and advise Harbor Circuit, a fictional esports organization, on one decision it should make in response. Treat Finch’s forecasts as possibilities to test, not as current facts. No outside research, gaming expertise, personal data or account is required.

This is ungraded planning. Complete the actual Knowledge Check in Canvas when your instructor posts it. Downloading notes is not submitting them. Your instructor supplies the deadline and submission route separately.

Use the three rounds below to test your thinking. For each round, choose a path and note your initial assumption. Then read the update and explain whether you keep, change or defer your recommendation, one trade-off, and what evidence you need.

## 1. Technology
Harbor can test one idea for six weeks: phone-friendly competitions, a headset-based experience, or AI-assisted match-clip selection. Which would you test, and what must be true for it to help?

Update: a proposed partner cannot confirm one needed capability or permission. Identify that dependency for your chosen path. Revise the test so it does not promise an unavailable feature.

## 2. Audience
Harbor has a growing free audience. Choose a small paid-membership offer, a creator collaboration, or one local community event. What behavior would show the idea has value?

Update: attention rises but repeat participation stays flat. Would you repeat, change or stop? Identify one useful measure, including cost where relevant.

## 3. Safeguards
Choose one safeguard to strengthen: competition rules, reporting concerns, or moderation responsibilities.

Update: participants find one rule confusing and do not know the contact person for concerns. Name who owns the response and explain how your chosen safeguard works with the other two.

## Final planning memo
In a short individual memo, explain your chosen Finch trend and why it could matter. Identify the audience and one unmet need, then recommend a small pilot Harbor Circuit should try. Explain one tradeoff and name the evidence you would use to expand, revise or stop the pilot. Treat growth and need as hypotheses. This prepares the final Canvas response; do not write a second essay. Follow the Canvas question's length and submission instructions when available.

Save your own notes. This page does not collect or submit your response.
