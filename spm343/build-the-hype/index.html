YOUR EVENT, TAKING SHAPE

An event is more than the bracket. Give people a reason to show up, stay, and come back.

12–15 minutes · Solo or pairs · No gradesIllustrative simulation · Choices stay in this browser

YOUR QUICK BRIEF

Make an event worth attending.

  1. Choose who your event is for and what success means.
  2. Pick a format that serves that purpose.
  3. Spend up to 10 planning credits on up to 3 event features.
  4. Handle one opening-day surprise.
  5. Explain your tradeoff. Replay with the same surprise if time allows.

Work alone or with one partner on one device. In pairs, one person drives and the other challenges the choices; switch at opening day.

These outcomes are invented teaching signals, not attendance forecasts, grades, or proof that your goal was achieved. No login, student name, or submission is needed. Download or print the recap only if your instructor asks.
