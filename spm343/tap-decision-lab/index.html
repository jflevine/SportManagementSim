The case brief

The manager’s brief

24college visitors
12PC stations
2floor hosts
$300incremental budget

Your assignment: recommend one welcome event for 24 college visitors making their first visit to this venue. First visit does not necessarily mean first time gaming. Their gaming experience is not yet confirmed.

What is fixed: the booking is 90 minutes: 10 for arrival, 70 for the program, and 10 to close. There are 12 ready-to-use gaming PCs, two included floor hosts, and 16 places in the social area. The room holds all 24 visitors and up to three staff. People can remain seated at inactive PCs during group introductions or the social close.

What the prices cover: each proposal includes the room, equipment, two hosts, and all planned materials or prizes. The $300 is the maximum spending allowance for this event. The only optional added cost is a third host at $75. No revenue forecast or additional purchasing is required.

What is already arranged: game access, appropriate permissions, accessible arrival routes, equipment checks, and a suitable game for each format. You do not need to choose a game or design a venue. Host 1 supports PC play; Host 2 manages timing, transitions, and the social area. You may explain a different division of work.

Whose interests compete: the operator wants repeat visits, the gaming club wants meaningful competition, and student affairs wants a welcoming experience. All three base proposals fit the supplied limits. Choose the benefit you think matters most.
