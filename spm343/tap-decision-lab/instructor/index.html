Which esports event should TAP host?

Grade the judgment.

Students compare three proposals, save an initial position, then adapt to supplied registration information. This is individual work, approximately 10–15 minutes, and independent of the guest interview.

10-point scoring guide
  1. Initial position · 2: identifies a reasonable initial choice (1) and supports it with a relevant venue or audience fact (1)
  2. Event and venue fit · 3: a feasible final plan within supplied limits (1), a purposeful adjustment (1), and an explanation of how the space, equipment, staff, or time supports it (1)
  3. Tradeoff and risk · 3: fairly weighs the runner-up’s benefit (1), identifies a stakeholder tradeoff (1), and gives a concrete risk with a practical response (1)
  4. Adaptation · 2: uses the new registration information (1) and explains why the final decision changed or stayed, including its practical consequence (1)

Award points for reasoning, not for selecting a predetermined winner. All three proposals can earn full credit. Resource checks are formative validation, not an automatic qualitative grade. Submitted, ungraded, and 0/10 are different states.
