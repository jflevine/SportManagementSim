The original draft and deal facts

Maya is a 20-year-old independent gaming streamer. Northline Esports is a fictional organization recruiting her to create team content. You are her business manager. The creator agreement and proposed addendum remain unsigned.

This case file combines selected draft terms, background facts, and pending requests. It is a summary of the deal materials, not a complete contract. Some terms deliberately leave Maya with issues to negotiate.

  1. Formation — draft condition: The creator agreement takes effect only when both Maya and Northline sign it.
  2. Duties — draft language: The recruiting message proposes 20 streaming hours monthly. The draft instead says Maya must “stream regularly” and create promotions “as requested.”
  3. Pay and term — proposed terms: Northline will pay Maya $5,000 per month during a six-month term, subject to the early-termination provision below.
  4. Content — proposed ownership clause: “Northline will own all content Maya creates during the six-month term, including content for her personal channel and projects unrelated to her team duties.”
  5. Identity — proposed addendum: “For six months after the agreement takes effect, Northline may use Maya’s name and the headshot she has selected in unpaid posts promoting Northline on the team’s social media accounts.”

    Maya’s selection of the headshot is separate from copyright permission: a freelance photographer owns the photo. Northline also wants a paid headset advertisement using it. Maya has not approved that ad, and no photographer license for it has been obtained.

  6. AI — earlier permission and new request: Under a separate earlier permission, Maya authorized one existing recording of her voice for one Northline launch video. That permission is independent of this unsigned creator deal. Northline now asks to train an AI voice model on the recording and create new sponsor advertisements in Maya’s simulated voice. Maya has not agreed to these new uses.
  7. Sponsors — existing commitment: Maya already has an exclusive headset endorsement agreement with SoundArc. Northline’s sponsor, PulseGear, sells headsets and keyboards. The proposed team deal contains no written exception preserving Maya’s SoundArc commitment.
  8. Exit — proposed termination clause: Northline may terminate the creator agreement at any time for any reason. Future monthly payments stop when the agreement ends.
File 5 is a separate “what if?” exercise. Imagine Maya negotiates a revised agreement guaranteeing all six months of pay, and both parties sign it. Northline then refuses to pay in breach of that revised agreement. Use those facts only to calculate the loss in File 5. For your final recommendation, return to the unsigned draft above, which allows early termination and stops future pay.
