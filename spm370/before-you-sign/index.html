The original draft

Maya is a 20-year-old independent gaming streamer. Northline Esports is a fictional organization recruiting her to create team content. You are her business manager. No part of the original draft is signed.

  1. Formation: No agreement is effective until both Maya and Northline sign.
  2. Duties: The recruiting message proposes 20 streaming hours monthly. The draft says “stream regularly” and create promotions “as requested.”
  3. Pay and term: $5,000 monthly during a six-month term.
  4. Content: Northline claims all content created during the term.
  5. Identity: A proposed addendum permits Maya’s name and approved headshot in team social posts for six months.
  6. AI: Maya has authorized one recorded launch message. Northline now requests model training and new generated sponsor ads.
  7. Sponsors: Maya has an existing SoundArc headset exclusivity deal. Northline’s PulseGear sponsor sells headsets and keyboards.
  8. Exit: Northline may terminate at any time for any reason, ending future monthly pay.

The loss exercise in File 5 uses separate, explicitly revised assumptions: six guaranteed months and wrongful termination. Apply those assumptions only to that exercise.
