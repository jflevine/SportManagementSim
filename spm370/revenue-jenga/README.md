# Revenue Jenga — Esports Business Model Stability Lab

A self-contained classroom simulation for SPM 370, Chapter 3: Esports Business Models.

## Learning purpose
Students allocate 100 revenue points across common esports revenue streams, experience one of six shocks, then use limited pivot capacity to rebuild the model while identifying a new dependency and the contract/legal instrument that becomes more important.

## Classroom flow
1. Pick a role: publisher, professional team, tournament organizer, or player/creator.
2. Allocate exactly 100 points.
3. Draw one of six sealed shocks.
4. Reallocate up to 20 emergency pivot points.
5. Identify the new dependency and relevant contract/legal instrument.
6. Use the resilience score as a discussion prompt, not a grade.

## Suggested placement
Immediately after the lecture slide “How Professional Esports Teams Get Paid” and before “Closed & Partnered Publisher Leagues.”
