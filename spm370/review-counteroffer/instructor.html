Rubric guidance

Recommendation / 4: a defensible position grounded in the offer, client priorities, and practical consequences. Course concept / 2: accurate application, not just a label. Reply / 4: a specific, workable proposal within authority (2) and a useful question about the priority issue (2).

A different commercial choice can earn full credit. One well-chosen issue is sufficient. Do not assign points for length or treat a selection as automatically correct. If the recommendation is to decline, assess the reply as the next move if talks continued.

Original-version fairness: Students who began the original task may submit a clause revision instead. Award up to 4 points for a specific, workable revision that resolves the selected uncertainty within authority. Do not require a question or message format, and do not penalize use of the original instructions. Browser drafts have no reliable start record; do not infer the task version from submission time. The shared response field may contain either format.
