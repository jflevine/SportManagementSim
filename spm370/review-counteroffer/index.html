Review the counteroffer

Advise Harbor Circuit. Decide which terms work for your client and clarify language that could cause problems.

Individual work · 10 points · About 12 minutes, including class discussion

Your client’s brief

You coordinate Harbor Circuit, a community gaming event in Philadelphia on November 14, 2026. To promote registration, you want adult creator Casey Brooks (@CaseyPlays) to publish one 45-second video by November 7 at noon Eastern. Harbor Circuit, Casey, and this transaction are fictional.

Your supervisor prefers a $500 fee and authorizes up to $600 total. You need permission to repost the video on Harbor Circuit’s Instagram account through November 14. Keeping that same repost visible through December 14 would be useful. You do not need paid advertising, exclusivity, or access to Casey’s account.

You may pay up to 50% at signing, credited toward the fee, and the balance within seven days after publication. You may agree to one consolidated revision round and respond within two business days. You cannot promise extra money or another booking. If no deal is reached, your staff can promote the event using their own content.

Casey’s counteroffer

Nothing has been accepted or signed. Read all six clauses before responding.

1 Video and publication

I will create one 45-second promotional video featuring the event date, location, registration link, and details you supply. I will publish it on my CaseyPlays Instagram account on November 7 at noon Eastern.

2 Fee and payment

The total fee is $600: $300 at signing, credited toward the total, and the remaining $300 within seven calendar days after publication. No other payment is included.

3 Review and timing

I will send a draft by noon on November 5. One consolidated revision round is included. Please respond promptly. If approval arrives late, publication may move until the video is ready.

4 Reposting and promotion

Harbor Circuit may repost the approved video once on its Instagram account by November 14 and leave that repost visible through December 14. Short excerpts may also be used in related event promotions.

5 Additional support

I will provide reasonable additional promotional support during event week if requested, as part of this fee.

6 Ownership and permissions

I retain ownership of my original material. I will use original footage and music licensed for my post and your agreed repost, and disclose restrictions before approval. No account access, paid advertising, or exclusivity is included.

Your advice

Write two short answers, about 120–180 words total. Concise bullets are welcome; this is guidance, not a word-count requirement. Address one priority ambiguity; you do not need to repair the whole agreement.

Use your own reasoning. Course readings and notes are allowed; generative AI is not. No outside research or formal citations are required.

1. What do you recommend?

Identify one term that works for Harbor Circuit and one unclear obligation. Use a contract concept from class to explain a practical consequence beyond price. If you propose different commercial terms, state them.

2. Make the obligation clear

Use a short, plain-English replacement that makes the obligation clear and workable. Stay within your authority. You do not need legal drafting language.
